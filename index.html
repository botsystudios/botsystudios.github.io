Quick answers

Frequently asked questions.

A few helpful details before you start a project with Botsy Studios.

How do I place an order?
Join our Discord server and open a ticket. Tell our team what you need, and we’ll discuss the requirements and next steps there.
Are the prices fixed?
No. Prices shown on this website are starting estimates. Your final quote depends on the features, complexity and scope of the project.
How do I pay?
Accepted payment methods and the final price are confirmed by our team in your Discord ticket before an order is approved.
Can I track my project?
Use the Client Portal to access the available client features. Project updates and communication are handled through your Discord ticket.
Can I request a custom system?
Yes. Open a ticket and describe your idea. Our team can review custom requirements and let you know what is possible.
