Questions

A few things you might be wondering.

What kind of projects does Botsy Studios take on?

We focus on custom Discord bots and server systems, Discord integrations, website development and related digital solutions. Share your idea and we can discuss whether it’s a good fit.

How do I request a project or get a quote?

Use the project enquiry option below to get in touch through our Discord support server. Tell us what you need, your goals and any important timing or requirements so we can understand the project.

How are project scope and pricing decided?

Pricing depends on the requirements, complexity and scope. We’ll discuss the details with you before work begins so expectations can be agreed clearly.

Can you work on an idea that isn’t listed here?

Yes. The services above are a starting point. If you have a digital challenge or a custom idea, get in touch and tell us what you’re aiming to achieve.

Do you have a client dashboard or live project tracker?

Not at the moment. Project updates and communication are handled directly with the team; dashboard features may be introduced in the future.
